# Growth playbook

**The hook: "Stop getting ghosted."**

Same people as DirectRoles, same kind of marketing, a direct problem. Almost everyone who has looked for a job knows the feeling: you apply, and nothing comes back. Not even a no. We don't have to explain it, we only have to name it.

The reason we give is just as easy to get: by the time a job reaches the big boards, hundreds of people have applied, and yours sits at the bottom. The fix is just as plain: get new roles the hour they go live on the company's own site, and be one of the first to apply.

## The facts we lean on (and only these)

| Claim | Source | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More than half of applications arrive in a job's first 4 days | Chicago Booth, Application Flows: 66 million applications | US boards, mostly tech and finance |
| Employers take applications for about 9 days on average | Same study | Same |
| 56 in 100 UK hiring managers say they'd stop replying to candidates | CV Genius, 625 UK hiring managers, Sept 2025 | A CV company's survey, what people said |
| N new UK roles went live in the last 24 hours | Our own data, counted live | Changes every hour |

We don't use the "8x more interviews in the first 96 hours" figure that circulates online. It comes from one company's analysis with no published study. If someone asks, we say so: it's part of the brand.

## Who we target

From the teardown, the people who pay are:

- **Unhappy in a job** (DirectRoles: 13.6 in 100 of these buy). Copy: "Get out before the crowd gets in."
- **Searching 3+ months** (6 to 7 in 100). Copy: "Stop getting ghosted", plus their own Ghost Count.
- **Seniors**. Copy: "Skip the queue. Go straight to the company."

Entry level and brand-new searchers convert badly (1.5 to 2.3 in 100), but they share the Ghost Count more than anyone. Treat them as the distribution, not the revenue.

## The three loops

1. **Ghost Count** (`/ghosted/...`, made at the end of the quiz and from the tracker). "Ghosted 130 times. 87 in 100 of my applications never got a reply." It downloads as a 9:16 story image. People post it because it says something about them, and anyone who reads it wants their own number. This is the lead hook.
2. **Is it worth applying?** (`/check`). Free, no sign-up. Paste a job and get "Apply today", "Long shot" or "Probably too late" with the real posting date. Every share shows the verdict as the preview image.
3. **Give a week, get a week**. Members' cards and links carry their code. A friend who joins gets a free first week, and the member gets a free week.

Behind them sits the data loop: every tracked application feeds company reply rates, so "Does X reply?" pages get better as more people use them.

## Content: hooks to film

Short, face to camera or screen recording: one feeling, one fact, one call to action. Every number comes from the live data on the day you film.

- "Applied to 100 jobs and heard nothing back? It's probably not your CV." (then the 4-day fact)
- "You're not getting rejected. You're getting ignored." (Ghost Count on screen)
- "My Ghost Count is 130. What's yours?" (story image, link to the quiz)
- "Stop being applicant number 500." (screen record a role posted 2 hours ago)
- "I pasted 10 jobs I applied to. Here's how many I was too late for." (the checker)
- "This role went live 40 minutes ago. Here's how I found it before LinkedIn." (only once we have measured that it's true for that role)
- "Over half of UK hiring managers admit they'd ghost you." (the CV Genius stat, with the source on screen)

Rules: never say a company posts fake jobs, never promise interviews, always show a real number.

## Paid ads

Same funnel as DirectRoles: ad, then straight into `/start`.

| Segment | Hook | Lands on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long searchers | "Applied to 100 jobs and heard nothing? See your Ghost Count." | `/start` |
| Unhappy in a job | "New jobs the hour they're posted. Be first in, quietly." | `/start` |
| Seniors | "Skip the queue. Apply straight to the company, first." | `/start` |
| Everyone (cheap reach) | "Is that job worth applying to? Paste the link." | `/check` |

Start on TikTok and Meta with UGC-style video. Track cost per paid member, not cost per click.

## PR

A monthly "Who's hiring right now" story from `/hiring-now`: which UK employers posted the most new roles, which sectors move fastest, how many live ads are over a month old. Pair it with the ghosting stats for a "why nobody replies" angle. Send it to HR and careers journalists, LinkedIn creators and student unions.

## Launch week

1. Day 1: the Ghost Count story image, ask people to reply with theirs. Pin it.
2. Day 2: "Applied to 100 jobs and heard nothing?" with the 4-day fact.
3. Day 3: the checker on 10 real jobs, live.
4. Day 4 to 7: one hook a day from the list, reply to comments with a check of the job they mention.
5. Turn on paid ads only once quiz completion and paywall numbers in `/admin` look healthy.

## Numbers to watch (in /admin)

- Landing to quiz start, and quiz drop-off per screen
- Email capture rate
- Paywall to paid, by segment and by A/B arm
- Trial to first full charge (£1 week vs free week)
- Week 2 and week 4 renewals on weekly plans
- Ghost Count shares and story downloads, checks per day, referrals that joined
- Refunds and cancel reasons

## Lines we don't cross

No fake reviews, no made-up member counts, no countdown that resets, no "was" prices we never charged, no spots-left counters, no statistics we can't source. Every number on the site is either counted live or linked to where it came from.
